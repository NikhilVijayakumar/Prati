# Design Review Practice

Review hierarchy, spacing, typography, states and consistency across light and dark themes. Check responsive behavior, keyboard and focus handling, readable text and localized content where applicable. Examine actual rendered output and identify the component, variant, environment and revision reviewed.

Separate review guidance from evidence: a checklist, design reference or historical audit does not prove a present implementation passes. Record skipped or unavailable checks and unresolved design decisions.
