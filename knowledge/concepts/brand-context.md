# Brand Context and Scope

Astra's documented design direction emphasizes quiet, precise interfaces, neutral surfaces and restrained accents. BAVANS materials describe a separate brand vision and a dark-first, cinematic website direction. The two sets of documents are references with distinct contexts; the existence of BAVANS materials does not establish that every Prati consumer must adopt BAVANS branding.

When brand choices differ, identify the target product and approved design reference before applying palette, logo, typography or motion decisions. Branding is not a substitute for semantic accessibility or consumer theming.

The BAVANS material is retained as scoped reference context. Its application to Prati itself is unresolved; no BAVANS-specific styling is assumed to be a Prati requirement.
